Honest by design. The scenarios here are simulated: the floods, spoofers and flight paths are staged so you can see a clear story. But every decoded field, trust verdict and detection result is produced by the open-source crates themselves, run natively against real payloads (including DJI captures from the NDSS 2023 research dataset) and replayed in the browser. Nothing is faked and no capability is invented.

The stack is receive-only. remoteid-sentinel and every demo on this page listen and decode. There is no transmit path anywhere in this code.
