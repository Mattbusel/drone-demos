What just happened. Standard Remote ID shows the drone. DJI DroneID also carries the operator's live GNSS fix, so remoteid decodes exactly where the pilot is standing — here, 18 m from the take-off point.
What you are seeing: every value is the real decode of one 91-byte OcuSync DroneID payload by remoteid::dji::FlightInfo::decode (CRC checked). The drone pin, home pin and pilot pin are the exact aircraft, home and operator-phone coordinates carried in that one frame. Nothing here is invented.
