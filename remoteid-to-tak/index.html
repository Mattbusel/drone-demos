What you are seeing

uas-cot bridges drone Remote ID into the military and first-responder TAK picture (ATAK, WinTAK, TAK Server). The left panel is the real broadcast as remoteid decoded it from a sample Wi-Fi / Bluetooth capture; the right panel is the real <event> Cursor-on-Target XML that uas-cot emits for it, the same bytes a TAK client parses.

A decode-only bridge paints every broadcast as a genuine aircraft. uas-cot instead writes the remoteid verdict into the marker: a spoofed transmitter becomes a suspect air track (a-s-A-M-F-Q) with Trust: spoofed (…) in its remarks, and its takeoff point gets its own marker instead of sending a responder to where the drone left from. Everything here is produced by the crates run natively on the captures; the scenarios are real sample captures, nothing is faked.
